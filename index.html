Before you convert

  1. Converting makes a modified copy. Open licenses like the OFL usually allow it; many commercial ones don’t.
  2. With a Reserved Font Name, rename the exports before sharing.
  3. Keep the copyright and license notices in the files.
  4. Check the license covers your use: web, app, print or redistribution.
